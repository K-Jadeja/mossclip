# Mossclip questions and answers

Answers are taken from the Mossclip website (https://mossclip.com/en). If this page and the website differ, the website is right. Back to the [README](../README.md).

## What is Mossclip?

**Mossclip turns Twitch, Kick and YouTube streams into Shorts and TikToks, and clips Twitch and Kick while you are still live.** Facecam on top, captions in your language, a whoosh to open and a vine boom on the funny moment, 3D emojis, and a share link for every clip. It also clips podcasts, interviews and vlogs, and opens every clip in an editor so the final cut is yours.

## Can Mossclip clip a Twitch or Kick stream while it is live?

**Yes.** Paste the live channel's link (`twitch.tv/name` or `kick.com/name`) while the stream is on, and Mossclip turns its latest two hours into Shorts before it ends. On a paid plan, tick Keep clipping and every new two hours becomes clips on its own until the stream ends. YouTube streams are clipped from the recording.

## Was Mossclip called something else?

Yes. Mossclip was called TwoReel until October 2026. Links to tworeel.com lead to mossclip.com.

## What can I give it?

A live Twitch or Kick channel, a Twitch or Kick recording link, a YouTube link, a Google Drive or Dropbox link, or an MP4 or MOV upload. Podcasts, interviews, streams, vlogs and stand-up all work. One job takes up to two hours, and when a linked recording is longer you choose which part to clip.

## My stream is longer than two hours. Can I clip all of it?

Yes. Paste the link of the whole recording and choose which two hours to clip: drag along the recording, press a chapter, or type the times. Then run another job for the next part, until the whole stream is covered.

## Do I need to download the recording or connect my Twitch, Kick or YouTube account?

**No.** For Twitch and Kick the channel link (while live) or the recording link is enough, so you can also clip streams from channels you do not own, as long as you have the right to clip them. A live YouTube stream is the one exception: paste its link once that stream has ended.

## How long are the clips, and what size?

Between 15 seconds and 3 minutes. Leave the length on auto, or choose a range before you start. Clips are exported at 1080 by 1920 with the captions burned in.

## Is there a watermark?

**No.** Every clip is exported at the full 1080 by 1920 with no watermark, on the free start too.

## Can I change what the AI did?

Yes. Every clip opens in the editor, where you can cut words out of the transcript, restyle or rewrite the captions, move the crops and export again.

## Does it work on gameplay streams?

Yes. Mossclip finds the facecam and stacks it over the gameplay, so a vertical clip keeps both the reaction and the play. Both a bordered camera panel and a streamer cut out over the game are handled. You do not have to mark where the camera is. Either crop can be moved and resized, saved as a layout, applied to the whole batch and chosen again for the next job. There is no list of supported games.

## How does it find the moments in a stream?

It reads what was said, listens for the loud moments and looks at what is on screen, so a clutch or a jump scare with nothing said is still found.

## Can I tell it what I want?

Yes. Say what you are after in a sentence before you start, or leave it on auto.

## Which languages do captions support?

**More than 30,** including English, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Russian, Serbian, Turkish, Arabic, French and German. Captions and titles follow the language the streamer speaks, even when the game is in another language.

## Can it add vine boom and whoosh sound effects automatically?

**Yes.** Every clip opens with a whoosh so the first second holds the viewer, and gets a vine boom on the punchline, the crash or the "oh no" moment when there is one. Pick another sound or turn it off before a job, or change it on any finished clip.

## Can I turn the headline on top on or off?

**Yes.** New project has a Headline on top switch: On puts a short hook line above every clip, Off leaves just the video and its captions.

## Can I share clips without uploading them anywhere?

**Yes.** Every finished clip has a Share button. It gives a link to a page that plays that one clip, so you can send it to the streamer or a friend before posting it anywhere. The link works for as long as the clip is kept, fifteen days.

## How many caption styles are there?

Nine: Karaoke, Beasty, Hormozi, Popline, Deep Diver, Bouncy, Highlighted, Mozi and Youshaei. Every word is timed to the speech, and any word can be retyped in the editor. The free [caption style preview](https://mossclip.com/en/tools/caption-style-preview) shows a line you type in all nine.

## How long do my clips stay available?

Fifteen days from the day they are made. Download the ones you want to keep.

## Can I use it for client work?

Yes, with footage you own or have permission to clip. The finished files are yours to deliver.

## How do I start?

**Anyone can sign up, with Google or an email link,** on the [sign-in page](https://mossclip.com/en/login). With Google, your first 30 minutes of video are free. Join the Mossclip Discord server and register your account email to raise that to two hours in total, plus two styled clips, once.

## How much does it cost?

**Starter $9 a month for 4 hours of video, Creator $29 a month for 13 hours, Studio $79 a month for 40 hours.** Creator is **$19 a month for as long as you stay** if you subscribe by 26 November 2026. With no subscription, **2 hours of video cost $5**, bought once, and they do not expire. Subscribe from the billing page in your account. The plans are on the [pricing page](https://mossclip.com/en/pricing) and in the [README](../README.md#pricing).

## Is it cheaper in my country?

**Often, yes.** In many countries the checkout shows a lower price for your country, up to 30% off. In India, Creator at the founding price is about ₹1,285 a month, plus tax. Studio is the same price everywhere. Prices are before tax: the checkout adds any sales tax, VAT or GST.

## Can I cancel, and can I get a refund?

You can cancel at any time from your billing page and keep the plan until the end of the period you paid for. Unused hours carry over for one month, even if you cancel. The first payment is refunded in full within 7 days if you have used less than a quarter of the allowance. The full rules are on the [refunds page](https://mossclip.com/en/refunds).

## How do I contact Mossclip?

Write to the [contact page](https://mossclip.com/en/contact). The site aims to reply within 3 working days. For a technical problem, send the link of the results page and describe the problem. You can also reach out on the Mossclip Discord server.
