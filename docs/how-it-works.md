# How Mossclip works

Mossclip turns a long video into short vertical clips. This page describes the four steps in more detail. The product is at https://mossclip.com/en. Back to the [README](../README.md).

## 1. Start with the long one

Paste a live Twitch or Kick channel, a YouTube, Twitch or Kick recording link, a Google Drive or Dropbox link, or upload the file (MP4 or MOV). Leave the rules on auto, or say what you are after in a sentence, for example "the moments where they talk about money". Leave the clip length on auto (15 seconds to 3 minutes) or choose a range before you start.

**Twitch and Kick work while you are live, or from the recording.** While the stream is on, paste the channel (`twitch.tv/name` or `kick.com/name`) and its latest two hours come back as clips before it ends; on a paid plan, Keep clipping does every new two hours until the stream ends. After the stream, paste the recording (for Twitch, the address with `twitch.tv/videos` in it; for Kick, the one from the channel's videos). There is nothing to download or upload and no account to connect. A live YouTube stream is clipped from its recording: paste the link once it has ended. Because Mossclip starts from the recording, where the whole stream is, it finds the moments for you instead of starting from one somebody already cut.

### Choosing the part of a long recording

One job takes up to two hours of recording. When a linked recording is longer, Mossclip shows the whole thing as one strip. You can:

- drag or resize the block along the strip,
- press a chapter (Twitch's game chapters, or a YouTube video's chapters), or
- type the start and end times into the two boxes.

A recording that fits in one job is taken whole, and you can still trim it. When you want another part, run another job. A recording of any length is covered two hours at a time.

## 2. Get a batch, not a guess

Mossclip reads the transcript and picks the moments. On gameplay streams it also listens for the loud moments and looks at what is on screen, so a clutch, a jump scare or a rage quit is found even when nobody says a clippable line. There is no list of supported games.

On the site's example job, Mossclip read a 59-minute interview (13,229 words), found 36 moments and kept the best 9. Each clip comes back cut vertical, captioned and titled.

### Gameplay streams: facecam over gameplay

A stream is laid out for a wide screen: the game fills it and the camera sits in a corner. Cropping the middle to 9:16 loses the camera, and following the face loses the game. Mossclip finds the camera, whether it sits in a bordered box or the streamer is cut out over the game, and stacks it on top with the gameplay below. You do not mark where the camera is.

Drag or resize either crop, save the layout, apply it to the whole batch, and choose it again when you start the next job. A podcast, an interview or a vlog, with no camera panel, gets a single crop that follows the person speaking.

## 3. Cut by deleting words

Every clip opens in the editor with its transcript beside the video. Select the words you do not want, press Backspace, and the clip is shorter by exactly those seconds. Captions follow the cut. A word the transcription got wrong can be retyped. You can also change the caption style, move a crop and export again.

## 4. Leave with a file

Export renders 1080 by 1920 with the captions burned in, between 15 seconds and 3 minutes long, with no watermark. Download one clip or the whole batch and post from your own accounts.

Clips stay available for 15 days from the day they are made. Download the ones you want to keep.

## Caption styles

Every word is timed to the speech, in a choice of nine styles: Karaoke, Beasty, Hormozi, Popline, Deep Diver, Bouncy, Highlighted, Mozi and Youshaei. The position can be top, middle or lower. A free page, [caption style preview](https://mossclip.com/en/tools/caption-style-preview), plays a line you type in each of the nine styles.

## Sound, headline, emojis and sharing

Every clip opens with a whoosh and gets a vine boom on the punchline when there is one; pick another sound or turn it off. A Headline on top switch on New project puts a short hook line above every clip, or leaves just the video and its captions. 3D emojis are drawn into the clip. Every finished clip has a Share button that gives a link to a page playing that one clip.

Captions and titles come in more than 30 languages and follow the language the streamer speaks, even when the game is in another language.
