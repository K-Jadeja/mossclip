# Changelog

Newest first. Entries from October come from the dated [changelog on the Mossclip website](https://mossclip.com/en/changelog). November entries are the dates the features went live on mossclip.com. The website is the source of truth.

## 23 November 2026

- **Regional prices.** Starter, Creator and the packs cost less in many countries, up to 30% off. In India, Creator at the founding price is about ₹1,285 a month, plus tax. Signed-in visitors see their country's price on the pricing page.
- **A one-step checkout.** The payment page shows the payment methods straight away and asks only for country and, where tax needs it, a postcode.

## 22 November 2026

- **Headline on top, On or Off.** New project asks whether every clip gets a short hook line above it, and keeps your answer.
- **Titles follow the captions' language.**

## 19 November 2026

- **Clip a Twitch or Kick stream while it is live.** Paste the live channel and its latest two hours become Shorts before the stream ends. On a paid plan, Keep clipping does every new two hours until the stream ends.
- **Whoosh and vine boom.** Every clip opens with a whoosh and lands a vine boom on the punchline when there is one. Both can be changed or turned off.
- **3D emojis** drawn into the clip.
- **A share link for every clip.** The Share button gives a page that plays that one clip.
- **Captions in the streamer's own language,** even when the game is in another one.
- GTA 6 pages: [GTA 6 streams to Shorts](https://mossclip.com/en/tools/gta-6-stream-to-shorts) and the [GTA 6 clipping guide](https://mossclip.com/en/guides/gta-6-clipping).

## 15 October 2026

- **A headline on every clip.** Each clip opens with its hook written across the top, emoji included.
- **The Headline layout.** The whole picture, the headline in the bar above it and the captions underneath.

## 14 October 2026

- **Short clips for campaigns.** A 15 to 35 second clip length for tight single moments.
- **Send clips straight to YouTube** as a Short from the Post button.

## 12 October 2026

- **Clips stay on the action when nobody is on camera.**

## 6 October 2026

- **Sign-up is open to everyone,** with Google or an email link.
- **Clips built from the whole story:** one clip can join the setup and the payoff.
- **Up to 12 clips from one long video.**
- **Drawn avatars** (VTubers, PNGtubers) get the camera-over-game layout.
- **Send a clip to your phone** with a QR code or a link.
- Also: framing that follows the video, viewers' Twitch clips help pick the moments, stock video in the editor, a Layout button in the editor, and clips that end on the payoff.

## 5 October 2026

- **Three looks:** Blackboard, Notebook and Collage.
- **Every clip scored and ranked,** best first.
- **Delete any finished project.**

## 4 October 2026

- **Links from Google Drive and Dropbox,** or a direct video address.
